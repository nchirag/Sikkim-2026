Sikkim 2026

Plan A · 24–31 December · Bagdogra → Darjeeling → North Sikkim → Gangtok → Zuluk → Kolkata · by bike

Before you go

  • Inner Line / protected area permits for North Sikkim (Lachung, Lachen, Yumthang, Zero Point, Gurudongmar): apply ahead and carry several photocopies of ID. Confirm at the Mangan tourism office.
  • Check with your bike rental whether the bikes (and their registration) are allowed into North Sikkim. Rules for outside vehicles change, so get this in writing.
  • December is winter at altitude: −10°C or colder at Lachen, Gurudongmar, Zero Point. Pack thermals, insulated gloves, balaclava, waterproof riding gear.
  • Carry spare tubes, a puncture kit, chain lube, a tow rope and cash (ATMs are scarce above Mangan).
  • Fuel up at Mangan or Gangtok; no pumps beyond Chungthang.
  • Book every stay ahead for 24–31 Dec: it is peak season, and Lachung/Lachen have very few rooms.
  • Road and weather: check the Sikkim police / BRO page and local drivers' groups each morning.
